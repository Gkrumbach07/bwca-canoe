# Theme A: fire, disturbance and forest (checked 2026-10-26)

"Box" means the game extent in `scripts/config.mjs` (lon −92.55 to −89.88, lat 47.78 to 48.40). "In BWCAW" means features that intersect the DNR 1978 BWCAW polygon, posted as the query geometry. Every count below came from a live REST query today.

**Answer to the owner's first ask:** no single source has every burn. The most complete set with years is the **USFS EDW Final Fire Perimeter (All Years)** layer: 99 in the box and 74 in BWCAW, covering 1923–2021. It has Pagami, Ham Lake, Cavity, Little Indian Sioux, John Ek and the rest.
- **NIFC's Interagency Fire Perimeter History** is a subset: 72 in the box, and it stops at 2016.
- **MN DNR's "Wildfires Tracked"** is points only and leaves out federal land: 2 points fall inside BWCAW, so it can't be used.
- **What EDW lacks:**
  - **The July 2026 fires:** a lightning storm on 7 July 2026 started fires over about 78,000 acres. They're in NIFC WFIGS only, as daily perimeters, and the Superior NF has a closure in force to 1 Apr 2027 (Forest Order 09-09-00-26-11).
  - **Prescribed burns:** these are in USFS FACTS and MTBS.
  - **Unnamed 1916–1942 fires:** these are in the Superior NF's own FireHistory layer, which is only online as university re-shares.

So: EDW, plus WFIGS for 2026, plus FACTS/MTBS for prescribed burns.

Two corrections to the brief:
- **Famine Lake** burned in **Sept 2006** (4,042 ac), not 2011.
- **Greenwood 2021** (26,829 ac) lies at lat 47.51–47.67, south of the box, so it isn't in the game world.

## Top 3 for theme A
1. **Real burns: EDW Final Fire Perimeter + WFIGS 2026 + FACTS/MTBS prescribed burns.**
   - **How it gets in (the bake):**
     - Add a `scripts/burns.mjs` step that queries the three REST layers in the bbox and keeps `{year, name, kind}`.
     - Rasterize "most recent burn year" (uint8, year − 1900) into a tile field.
     - `burnAt` then reads that field instead of fbm, with a fade by age so 1936 reads as mature and 2026 as black.
   - **Smaller first step:** about 120 polygons simplified to about 20 m fit in a `src/burns.json` runtime table, under 0.3 MB gzipped, with point-in-polygon at load.
   - **Size:** source under 1 MB.
2. **The 1999 blowdown and other disturbance: MN DNR fast forest disturbances v4.**
   - **How it gets in (the bake):** filter to `agent=6`, years 1999–2001, plus harvest outside the wilderness. Rasterize "blowdown year" next to the burn year, for downed-log density and the young aspen/birch/fir regrowth that followed.
   - **Size:** about 10 MB GeoJSON source, a few hundred KB baked.
3. **Forest type and age: LANDFIRE LF2024 EVT (+ EVH height), then Heinselman stand-origin years.**
   - **How it gets in (the bake):**
     - EVT replaces the noise forest type and gives real bogs and rock (three 30 m rasters, about 15 M px each, under 30 MB source).
     - Heinselman, once provenance is cleared with the Superior NF or UMN, gives stand age back to 1610 under the modern burns (6 MB GeoJSON source).
   - **Size:** both bake to a byte per 80 m far cell, small.

## Sensitive data: don't use or publish
- **Tree-ring fire-scar sample sites** (UMN "BWCAW Tree Ring Fire History", "Earliest_Fires_Comparison", the USFS "Fire Scar Citizen Science Survey" form): exact locations of living fire-scarred old pines. Use the fire *years* only, never the sites. **No.**
- **Archaeological sites** (e.g. the DRUM report on a Knife Lake siltstone quarry) and any logging-camp or Ojibwe site locations: **no.**
- **Wildlife den and nest locations** turned up nearby (DRUM "Den sites of radiocollared Canada Lynx 2004–2007"): **no.**
- **Rare-species and rare-plant locations:** the DNR NHIS rare-features database is licensed and not public. Don't seek it. The NPC/MBS layers above are public community maps, not rare-species points. **No.**
- **Human-caused fire details** (WFIGS point of origin and cause investigations, DNR wildfire points with private-property loss): keep perimeters, years and names only.
