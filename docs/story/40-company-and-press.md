# The company and the press

Who works for Northmark today and who doesn't; how the company closes in as the player gets closer; the journalist,
from her first note to the meeting point; what people say at each stage; and the midpoint scares written as full
scenes.

Canon is `00-owner-decisions.md`, then `01-bible.md`. This file adds detail inside them and lists every invention under
the last heading. Voices follow `02b-voice-people.md` (sections 3.3, 6.10, 6.11) and `02a-voice-journal.md`. Places and
ids are in `03-places.md`. House style is British spelling.

What this file does **not** cover, because other story files own it: the Kettle pad set piece and Tauno's, Mary's and
Len's arc scenes (the mining crew); Marlys, Hap and Sheila (the guiding years); the boys and the informant reveal; the
family; the run itself; the dawn lot and the endings. Where those touch the company or Maggie, this file gives only the
company's or Maggie's side.

**How to read the conversations.** Each one gives its **condition** (what has to be true for it to happen), an
**opener**, the player's **choices** (short, in bold), the **replies**, and what each branch **gives**: a lead, a pencil
ring, an item, a line on the Walt page, or a change in a story flag. Lies are marked **LIE**, with the **tell** that lets
a careful player catch them. Stage directions for designers are in [brackets]. Walt page lines are in the player's own
words, as the bible requires.

**Needling Brekke** (3.5) steps the watchers up one tier at once, but never past tier 4: only the cellar starts the run.
The step changes the watchers' presence (sightings, trucks, the man at the landings); the scripted scenes of each tier
(the search, the counter, the put-in, the warning) still wait for their own triggers.

**What each step is for.** A designer's key, never said in game:
- **The watching** learns where the player goes, from a distance.
- **The stranger at the far landing** is a watcher who misjudged and was caught waiting; Wade moves him to another
  lake.
- **The camp search** checks whether the player already has the papers. They don't; the notebook is on them.
- **The counter and the put-in** are Wade letting the player know he knows them. He enjoys it a little.
- **The warning** is Wade's own idea, not Brekke's: make the player hurry and get careless.
- **The offers** are Brekke's: buy it if it can be bought. Dan doesn't know what he is buying.

## 3. The company's scenes

### 3.1 Dan at the counter (tier 1)

**Condition.** Tier 1. The first time the player walks into any outfitter after tier 1 begins, Dan is at the counter
buying a coffee and chatting with the keeper. Once. If the player walks straight past him, he catches them at the door.

[Dan: forty-one, a Northmark fleece with the logo small on the chest, hockey-dad sunglasses pushed up on his head. He
is the only person in the game who talks to the player before they speak.]

**Opener.**
DAN: "You must be the one with the old outfitter gear. Small town. I'm Dan. I'm with the company."
[He holds out his hand. If the player is carrying Walt's pack, he glances at the BRM-14 patch and does not mention it.]

- **"Which company?"**
  DAN: "Northmark. The new sign at the junction. It's been up ten years and everybody still calls it the new sign."
  *(gives: the fact "Dan Marolt, Northmark, community relations")*
- **"How do you know who I am?"**
  DAN: "You've got a pack older than me with a crew stencil on it. People notice old gear up here. I noticed."
  DAN: "Also my sister-in-law does the books at two of these places. I'm kidding. Mostly."
- **"What do you want?"**
  DAN: "Nothing. Coffee. To say hi. We sponsor the peewee team, we do the food shelf in November. I like to know who's
  around."
- **"Did you know a guide named Walt?"** [Only if the player raises Walt. Dan never says the name first.]
  DAN: "Walt? Sure, I knew of him. Sad thing. Those big lakes in October."
  DAN: "My dad knew him a little. Said he was the only guide in Ely who'd take a client back to the same lake two days
  running if the fish were there."
- **"What about Boreal?"** [Only if the player has heard the name.]
  DAN: "The old outfit. Before my time. Way before. There's a lot of old stories about the company back then. Most of
  them are about men who're dead now."
  *(He believes this. It is half true.)*
- **"Are you for the mine?"**
  DAN: "My grandpa was underground at the Pioneer thirty years. My kids want to stay up here. So, yeah. Done right, I'm
  for it."
  DAN: "Ask me if I'd want it done wrong. I wouldn't. That's not a speech, that's my kids."
- **"I'm just here to paddle."**
  DAN: "Best reason there is."

**Close.** Every branch ends here.
DAN: "Listen, if you come across any old junk from the drill days, core, a casing, one of those old crew stencils,
bring it by. We're putting a little history wall up at the office. I'll pay you for it. Fair."
[He gives his card. Item: **Dan's card** (front: *Dan Marolt · Community Relations · Northmark Metals · Ely* and a phone
number; back, in ballpoint: *Anytime. —D.*). The card is what the money ending's permit-box phone calls (bible 7.2).]
DAN: "Can I buy you a coffee? No? Another time. I'm around."

**After he leaves**, the keeper, once:
KEEPER: "That's Dan. He's all right. He coached my nephew. Don't sell him anything you'd miss."

**Walt page:** "Dan Marolt. Company. Nice. Gave me his card."
